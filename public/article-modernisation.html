The premise.

The largest employers in Southeast Asia aren't tech companies. They are family-owned groups with thirty-year operating histories, tens of thousands of employees, spreadsheets held together with willpower, and a founder-generation transition that is either underway or imminent. These businesses don't need a digital transformation consultancy. They need an operator who has shipped software at scale and can tell the difference between a workflow worth modernising and one worth deleting.

What "modernisation" means here.

Not a new ERP. Not a four-year SAP program. Not a Gartner report. We look at the 20 workflows your operating teams hate most, pick the three where AI agents can carry real load, and ship them into production inside the engagement. The other 17 get a written recommendation and a priority order.

"Digital transformation" is what people say when they want to spend money. "Modernisation" is what they do when they want to stop spending it.

The engagement, in three acts.

  1. Weeks 1–3 — Diagnose. We shadow operators across your core business units. Finance close, procurement, compliance reporting, supplier onboarding, HR intake, customer ops. Every manual handoff logged.
  2. Weeks 4–10 — Ship. Three production-grade replacements, one per business unit. Each one handed to a named owner inside your team, with evals and a dashboard.
  3. Weeks 11–16 — Hand over. We train your engineers or operators to extend the systems. You own the repos, the prompts, and the roadmap.

Typical first wins.

  • 01
    Finance close acceleration. Monthly closes that take 14 days cut to 4. Agent reconciles AP/AR, flags anomalies, drafts the narrative.
  • 02
    Procurement & supplier onboarding. The 8-page PDF intake replaced by a WhatsApp intake agent that validates documents and books the site visit.
  • 03
    Compliance & reporting. Regulatory filings drafted from system-of-record data, reviewed by your team, filed on time without a 72-hour scramble.
  • 04
    Internal knowledge. A retrieval layer on top of 20 years of SOPs, contracts, and board packs — so your next hire doesn't spend six months figuring out what's already been decided.
