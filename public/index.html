Questions

Before you
book a call.

The questions founders ask most. Anything else, ask me on the call.

Is our data safe?

The system is set up in your company's own accounts, not mine. Nothing goes out without a person approving it, every action is logged, and you own all of it at handover. We agree in writing what the agents can and can't touch.

Do we need a tech team?

No. I set it up and train your team. If your people use email and a chat app, they can use this.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Copilot?

Those tools wait for you to ask. This system goes through what's open every morning, chases the owners, drafts the replies and brings you only the decisions that need you, each with options and a recommendation.

What if my team doesn't use it?

It's built around how your team already works, in the tools they already use. Training is part of the six weeks, and the hand-over only happens once it's running in daily use.

You have a C-level role. How much time do you have?

Enough for a few engagements at a time, which is why I keep it to a few. Each scope is realistic about my hours, and the agents handle much of the follow-through, including mine.

What does it cost?

A fixed fee, quoted in writing after a 30-minute call. It depends on team size and scope. There's no hourly billing and no open-ended retainer unless you want one.
