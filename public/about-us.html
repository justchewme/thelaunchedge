About The Launch Edge.

The Launch Edge is a Singapore-based management and growth consultancy. It helps founders and CEOs of growing companies run their business better: clearer decisions, faster growth, and systems their own teams can run.

BusinessManagement & growth consultancy
Based inSingapore
ClientsFounders & CEOs, seed to mid-size
FounderJustin Chew

What we do.

  • 01
    Leadership operating system. A six-week engagement that sets up a working system for a CEO and their leadership team: a daily brief, decision tracking, drafted replies and follow-ups with owners, inside the tools the team already uses. The client's team is trained and owns it at the end.
  • 02
    Fractional growth & product leadership. Senior growth and product leadership, about a day a week, for consumer and fintech companies that are not yet ready for a full-time executive hire: subscriptions, retention, activation, loyalty and payments.
  • 03
    Market entry. Helping companies choose and enter their next market in Southeast Asia, Europe or Taiwan: market choice, adapting the product to local rules, the first hire and the launch plan.
  • 04
    Advisory. Smaller engagements by conversation: modernising manual workflows in family businesses, private counsel for founders, and on-call advice.

How we work.

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute call, followed by a one-page written scope and a fixed quote. There is no hourly billing. The work is done directly by the founder, with no hand-off to a junior team, and it ends with a handover so the client owns what was built.

Who we work with.

Founders, CEOs and leadership teams of growing companies, mainly in Singapore and Southeast Asia, and also in Europe. Clients find us through referrals, LinkedIn and the essays published on this site.
