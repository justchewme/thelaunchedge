Advisory
Retainer.

On-demand Slack, warm intros, quarterly reviews. For seed founders, operators, and VCs who want us in their corner without a full engagement. A small number of slots — by referral or intro call only.

Duration12 months
CadenceAsync + quarterly
SlotsCapped · by referral
ModelFlat monthly

What's included.

  • 01
    Shared Slack channel. 24-hour turnaround on the questions you'd otherwise post to a half-asleep group chat. Draft pricing, a hire dossier, a cold outreach template — send it over.
  • 02
    Warm introductions. Into our operator network across Southeast Asia, Europe, the Gulf, and ANZ. We don't broker deals — we vouch for people, and only when the fit is real.
  • 03
    Quarterly review. One 90-minute session per quarter. Business review, decision audit, the next quarter's three priorities on a single page.
  • 04
    Ad-hoc reviews. A pitch deck, a fundraise term sheet, a key hire's comp package — bring it, we'll read it, you'll have comments back in 48 hours.

Who this fits.

Seed-stage founders who want senior operating instinct in the room without a full retainer. Product and growth leaders between jobs who want a sounding board while they run their next search. Emerging-market VCs who want a second read on operator-quality for portfolio companies across SEA.

Who it doesn't.

If your month looks like a crisis response, this isn't the right shape — we'll point you toward Principal Counsel or a Flagship engagement. If you need someone to ship the work, not just pressure-test it, that's Flagship too. Advisory is high-signal, low-surface-area. Both sides win when the box is shaped correctly.

The best advice is the email you didn't send, the hire you didn't make, and the market you chose not to enter this quarter. Advisory is sized for that kind of work.

How the year runs.

  1. Month 0 — Fit call. 30 minutes. If the fit is wrong, we say so on the call. If it's right, the retainer starts the following Monday.
  2. Month 1 — Onboarding. We read your data room, talk to two of your people, and write the first quarterly plan.
  3. Months 2–12 — Cadence. Slack, intros, quarterly reviews. We step into Flagship only if a specific project warrants it — and only by your invitation.

Ask for a referral or intro.

New slots open at the start of each quarter. Referrals from the network jump the line.
