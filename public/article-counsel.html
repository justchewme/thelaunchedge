Who this is for.

Founders, CEOs and C-suite executives carrying decisions they can't put in the shared channel. The call before the board call. The draft of a difficult conversation with a co-founder. The hire where you already know the answer and want someone to pressure-test it. The market entry you're not ready to brief the leadership team on.

Counsel is not therapy, not coaching in the HR sense, and not a board seat. It's a weekly 45 minutes to think out loud with a senior operator whose only stake in the outcome is your clarity.

Some of the most expensive decisions a leader makes happen alone, between meetings. This is a room for that conversation.

What a quarter looks like.

  1. Intake. A 90-minute session to map the live decisions, the standing tensions and the deadlines on the calendar. Nothing leaves the room.
  2. Weekly sessions. 45 minutes, same day every week, by video or in person in Singapore. You set the agenda the day before.
  3. Async access. A direct channel for decisions that can't wait a week. A reasonable response time, not a 24/7 hotline.
  4. Quarterly reset. We re-scope. You renew, step down to the advisory retainer, or stop. No auto-renewal.
