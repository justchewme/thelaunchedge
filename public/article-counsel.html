Who this is for.

CEOs, founders, and C-suite executives carrying decisions they cannot put in the shared channel. The call before the board call. The draft of the co-founder conversation. The hiring decision where you already know the answer and need someone to pressure-test it. The market entry you're not ready to brief the leadership team on.

Counsel is not therapy, it is not coaching in the HR sense, and it is not a board seat. It is the weekly forty-five minutes where you get to think out loud with a senior operator who has made, or sat beside, the decision you are about to make — and who has no stake in the outcome other than your clarity.

The most expensive decisions a CEO makes happen in silence between the board meeting and the bedroom. This is the room for that conversation.

What a quarter looks like.

  1. Intake. A 90-minute session to map the live decisions, the standing tensions, and the forcing functions on the calendar. Nothing leaves the room.
  2. Weekly sessions. 45 minutes, same day every week, video or in-person in Singapore. Agenda set by you, by 5pm the day before.
  3. Async access. Encrypted channel for decisions that can't wait a week. Reasonable response time, not 24/7 concierge.
  4. Quarterly reset. We sit down and re-scope. You renew, step down to Advisory, or walk. No auto-renewal.

What we bring to the room.

  • i.
    Operator history. Product & growth leadership at Gotrade (YC S19, 150+ countries), Foodpanda, and Delivery Hero (70+ countries). We've been inside the fundraise, the restructure, and the market entry you're facing.
  • ii.
    Institutional context. Partner at Reforge. Product Coach at Product School. Direct lines into the senior operator network across Southeast Asia, Europe, and ANZ.
  • iii.
    No conflict of interest. We do not invest, we do not recruit, and we do not take referral fees. Our only product is the quality of your next decision.

What Counsel is not.

× Not this

  • A coaching engagement built around a framework
  • A board seat or an observer seat
  • An introduction service for fundraising
  • A retained lawyer, therapist, or crisis PR firm

✓ This instead

  • A confidential operating room for one
  • Weekly 45-minute sessions, agenda-driven
  • A principal who's made the decision you're making
  • A clean quarterly exit if it's not working

Access.

Counsel is capped at a small number of seats. New seats open by referral, at the start of each quarter, after a private introduction call. If the fit is wrong — for us or for you — we will say so on the call, and we'll suggest the right alternative.
